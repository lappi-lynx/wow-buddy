1. Balance Druid (Caster DPS)
Resource Limits & Spells: Caster form relies heavily on Mana, and spell growth rates for Wrath (ranks 1–5) have been adjusted so higher ranks remain consistent upgrades
1
. Open with ranged spells and DoTs, then finish enemies in melee or Bear Form to allow your Spirit to regenerate mana outside the 5-second rule.
Mob Pull Size: Stick strictly to 1–2 mobs at a time in Leather/Cloth armor, as taking direct physical hits causes spell pushback and forces high-cost emergency healing spells.
Professions & Weapon Upgrades: Take Herbalism + Alchemy for mana potions and spell elixirs, or Fishing for campfire aquarium buffs that grant +8% to all stats
2
. Equip two-handed staves stacking Spell Power, Intellect, and Spirit (Gritroot Staff at lvl 10, Oakthrush Staff at lvl 12, Living Root at lvl 18, Staff of Westfall at lvl 20, and Odo's Ley Staff at lvl 30)

3. Restoration Druid (Healer)
Resource Limits & Spells: Apply HoTs before taking damage, then step back or shift into form to let your Spirit regenerate mana. The Natural Instinct talent converts 12/25% of Intellect into Spell Healing, giving Intellect gear extra healing power for Restoration builds
