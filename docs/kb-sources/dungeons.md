Typical Clear Time (Beginner Group): 60–90 minutes (owing to its large, winding cave layout and multi-boss escort event)
4 Beginner Survival & Etiquette Rules:
Pull Back from Open Tunnels: Always pull raptors and vipers backward into cleared areas. Humanoids and beasts in WC often run away when low on health and will quickly pull nearby sleeping or patrolling packs if not slowed or killed in time.
Interrupt & Dispel Druids of the Fang: Assign priority interrupts to Druids of the Fang casting Sleep (which incapacitates your tank/healer) and Healing Touch. Bring Poison Cleansing (potions or class abilities) for heavy nature DoTs.
Stay Together During the Disciple Escort: During the final ritual escort at the dungeon entrance, stay stacked near the Disciple NPC. Let the tank grab aggro on the incoming ambush waves before using heavy AoE.
Establish Need vs. Greed Rules for Hybrid Drops: WC contains iconic gear usable by multiple classes (e.g., agility leather, caster staves, shield drops). Communicate with hybrid specs (like Druids or Shamans) before rolling Need on off-spec gear.
2. Shadowfang Keep (SFK)
Recommended Level Range: Level 18–25
Typical Clear Time (Beginner Group): 45–60 minutes (a linear, multi-floor castle run)
4 Beginner Survival & Etiquette Rules:
Line-of-Sight Pull on Staircases: Use line-of-sight (LoS) pulling around doorways or down narrow stairwells in the courtyard so ranged guards and fleeing worgen don't pull extra packs above.
Coordinate Interrupts for Archmage Arugal: Save your interrupts and silence abilities for Arugal's high-damage Voidbolt casts
