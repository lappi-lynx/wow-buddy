## Question (verbatim)

> For WoW Forever 1.60.1: confirm the fishing basics with exact steps — (1) where do you learn Fishing (is there a fishing trainer, in which cities/towns?), (2) what do you need before casting (a fishing pole? bait/lure?), (3) how does actually fishing work (stand by water, cast, wait for the bobber)? And (4) is cooking the fish worth it while leveling — does Cooking turn caught fish into food that reduces downtime? Say plainly if anything is not known.

## Answer (verbatim, condensed)

> **1. Where to Learn Fishing** — Apprentice & Journeyman: at character level 5 you can learn Apprentice Fishing (up to 75 skill) from Fishing Trainers in all capital cities and near starting zones; at level 10 capital-city trainers teach Journeyman (up to 150). **Expert** (150–225): at level 20 buy the "Expert Fishing – The Bass and You" book for 1 gold from the fishing vendor in Booty Bay. **Artisan** (225–300): at level 35 with 225 skill, a quest from Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh.
> **2. Requirements** — You must equip a Fishing Pole in your main-hand slot; standard poles are sold by fishing/trade vendors. Baits/lures are not strictly required for basic casting but apply temporary skill bonuses and are necessary in higher-level zones so fish do not escape. Lures come from trade vendors or Engineer-crafted items (Aquadynamic Fish Attractors).
> **3. How Fishing Works** — Stand facing any fishable water; activate the Fishing spell to launch the lure; a bobber appears — watch or listen for the splash, then right-click it before the channel ends to reel in the fish or floating crate.
> **4. Is Cooking Worth It?** — Yes: Cooking raw fish turns them into food that restores health and mana, directly minimizing downtime. In WoW Forever, **cooked food grants a +5% experience gain buff**, and cooked fish dishes can also grant temporary fishing skill. Fishing and Cooking level together: fish caught leveling Fishing 1–150 suffice to level Cooking to ~150 without extra meat farming.
