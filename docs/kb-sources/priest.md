3. Shadow Priest (DoTs & Mana Regeneration)
Resource Limits & Spells: Repeatedly casting Mind Blast, Mind Flay, and DoTs will drain your mana pool if you don't weave in wanding. Cast Shadow Word: Pain early, use Mind Blast for initial damage, and rely on Spirit Tap talent procs upon killing enemies to rapidly refill your mana pool while finishing targets with Auto-Wand attacks.
Mob Pull Size: Safely handles 1–2 mobs at a time. Use Mind Flay to slow approaching melee enemies, keep Power Word: Shield active to prevent spell pushback, and use Psychic Scream to fear a secondary target away while executing the first.
Professions & Gear: Pair Tailoring + Enchanting to craft cloth gear and high-DPS Wands. Prioritize gear with Shadow Spell Power, Intellect, and Spirit, and upgrade your Wand every few levels—wand attacks during the final 30–50% of an enemy's health bar form the backbone of smooth Shadow leveling.
