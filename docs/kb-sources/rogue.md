2. Combat Rogue (Direct Melee Brawler)
Resource Limits & Mechanics: Combat relies heavily on auto-attack DPS and high weapon damage. Use Sinister Strike as your primary Energy spender, keep Slice and Dice up constantly for maximum attack speed, and pool Energy before activating burst cooldowns like Adrenaline Rush or Blade Flurry.
Mob Pull Size: Safely handles 1–2 mobs (and 3 mobs when cooldowns are ready). Activate Blade Flurry to cleave two enemies simultaneously, and use Gouge or Blind to incapacitate an extra target.

3. Subtlety Rogue (Stealth, Control & Mobility)
Resource Limits & Stealth Openers: Subtlety relies on high-impact stealth openers (Ambush, Cheap Shot) that consume large amounts of Energy upfront. Pool your Energy while stealthed before opening so you can follow up with combo builders without stalling out.
Mob Pull Size: Pull 1 mob at a time from stealth. Subtlety provides unmatched single-target lockdown through stuns (Cheap Shot, Kidney Shot) and incapacitates (Sap, Gouge), allowing you to take virtually zero damage while finishing off a target.
Professions & Weapon Upgrades: Choose Skinning + Leatherworking for easy armor upgrades or Engineering for utility tools and bombs. Prioritize a high-damage Dagger in your Main Hand for massive Ambush and Backstab openers, and stack gear with Agility, Stamina, and Critical Strike Chance.
