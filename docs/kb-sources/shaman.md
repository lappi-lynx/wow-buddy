## Answer (verbatim)

Thoughts
Here are 3 short, concrete beginner leveling tips for each Shaman specialization in WoW Forever, covering resource management, mob pull sizes, professions, and weapon/gear progression:
1. Elemental Shaman (Caster DPS)
Resource Limits & Spells: Mana drains rapidly when casting high-cost spells (Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Shocks). Because low-rank spell downranking is heavily nerfed in WoW Forever, always cast your highest available ranks of offensive spells. Drop mana-efficient totems (Searing Totem, Mana Spring Totem) and weave in melee hits or shocks so your Spirit can regenerate mana via the 5-second rule.
Mob Pull Size: Stick strictly to 1–2 mobs at a time. Taking melee hits causes heavy spell pushback on your cast times; use Earthbind Totem or Stoneclaw Totem to slow and distract additional enemies while you burst down your primary target.
Professions & Gear: Pair Skinning + Leatherworking (to craft Leather and Mail gear with caster stats) or Herbalism + Alchemy (for mana potions and spell power elixirs). Equip a 1H Mace/Dagger + Shield or a Staff stacking Spell Power, Intellect, and Spirit (Walking Stick at lvl 10, Staff of Westfall at lvl 20, or Odo's Ley Staff at lvl 30).
2. Enhancement Shaman (Melee DPS)
Resource Limits & Mechanics: Enhancement relies on Mana to cast weapon imbues (Rockbiter, Flametongue, Windfury Weapon), totems, and shocks. In WoW Forever, the old "hybrid tax" has been removed, bringing hybrid melee DPS within 5% of pure damage classes. Avoid spamming shocks on every cooldown—rely primarily on auto-attacks and weapon imbue procs to conserve mana.
Mob Pull Size: Comfortably handles 1–2 mobs at a time. You can cleave two enemies using Fire Nova Totem or Magma Totem, but pulling 3+ mobs will force you to cast emergency heals (Healing Wave), draining your mana pool instantly.
Professions & Gear: Take Mining + Engineering for grenades and dynamite pulls, or Skinning + Leatherworking. Equip slow, high-damage 2H Weapons (Heavy Copper Maul at lvl 12, Smite's Mighty Hammer at lvl 20, Corpsemaker at lvl 30) or Dual Wield 1H Axes/Maces. Orcs (+1% crit with axes) and Dwarves (+1% crit with maces) gain direct critical strike bonuses from their racial weapon choices
