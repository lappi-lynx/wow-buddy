Resource Limits & Sustain Engine: Maintain a continuous Health-to-Mana conversion loop using Life Tap alongside Drain Life, Siphon Life, and Corruption. Make sure to finish dying mobs with Drain Soul to keep a steady supply of Soul Shards for Healthstones and demon summons.
Mob Pull Size: Comfortably pull 2–3 mobs at once.
