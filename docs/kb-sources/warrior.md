## Answer (verbatim)

Thoughts
Here are 3 short, concrete beginner leveling tips for each Warrior specialization in WoW Forever, covering resource management, mob pull sizes, professions, and weapon progression:
1. Arms Warrior (2H Melee DPS)
Resource Limits (Rage): Arms generates Rage from slow, heavy two-handed swings and critical strikes. Avoid spamming Heroic Strike when low on Rage, as it replaces your next auto-attack and pauses passive Rage generation.
Mob Pull Size: Stick to 1–2 mobs at a time. Arms can handle two enemies using Sweeping Strikes and Cleave, but pulling 3+ mobs before obtaining heavy Plate armor will drain your health faster than you can generate Rage.
Professions & Gear: Pair Mining + Blacksmithing to craft Mail/Plate armor and sharpening stones. Prioritize slow 2H weapons (Heavy Copper Maul at lvl 12, Rhahk'Zor's Hammer at lvl 16, and Smite's Mighty Hammer at lvl 20). Humans (+2% crit with swords) and Orcs (+1% crit with axes) gain direct critical strike bonuses from their racial weapon choices.
2. Fury Warrior (Dual-Wield DPS)
Resource Limits (Rage): Dual-wielding generates a steady stream of Rage from frequent off-hand hits and crits. In WoW Forever, Whirlwind strikes with both equipped weapons simultaneously, but missing attacks due to the dual-wield hit penalty can cause sudden Rage starvation.
Mob Pull Size: Pull 1 mob at a time. Fury deals excellent single-target burst, but taking hits from multiple mobs without a shield will quickly force you to stop and eat.
Professions & Gear: Take Mining + Engineering for dynamite and utility grenades to pull or interrupt enemies from range. Equip two high-DPS 1H weapons (Swords, Axes, Maces, or Daggers), leveraging direct racial weapon critical strike bonuses.
3. Protection Warrior (Tank)
Resource Limits (Rage): Protection generates Rage primarily by taking damage in Defensive Stance. In WoW Forever, damage absorb shields no longer reduce incoming Rage gains, ensuring smooth Rage flow while shielded.
Mob Pull Size: Pull 2–3+ mobs at a time. Mitigate incoming physical damage with Shield Block and Revenge, and apply Demoralizing Shout—which properly generates threat on all nearby targets.
Professions & Gear: Choose Mining + Blacksmithing to craft heavy armor, shields, and shield spikes, alongside First Aid for essential self-healing. Pair a sturdy Shield with a fast 1H weapon to rapidly stack Sunder Armor and hold threat on targets.
