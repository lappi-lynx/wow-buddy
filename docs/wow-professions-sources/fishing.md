## How to fish (1–75), as captured

- Buy a Fishing Pole (item 6256) and a few Shiny Baubles (6529) from a Fishing/Trade Supply
  vendor; in capital cities they stand next to the trainer.
- Open the Professions window; click the **hammer button** in the right-side button row,
  **not the Fishing tab**; drag the Fishing button (spell 7731) to an action bar; equip the
  pole and apply the lure; stand in front of water in a starting zone and cast; mouse over
  the bobber and right-click the splash; fish to 75.
- Starting zones are **not** capital cities.

## Tier route (as captured)

- **1–75:** starting zones (Dun Morogh, Durotar, Elwynn Forest, Mulgore, Teldrassil, Tirisfal
  Glades, Zephras Isle); Shiny Bauble.
- **75–150:** Journeyman (Fishing 50 + level 10, same trainer); capitals (Darnassus, Stormwind,
  Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff) or The Barrens, Darkshore, Ironforge, Loch Modan,
  Silverpine Forest, Westfall; Nightcrawlers (6530, needs Fishing 50) if fish get away.
- **150–225:** Expert Fishing – The Bass and You (item 16083, level 20 + Fishing 125) from Old
  Man Heming in Booty Bay (or the Auction House); Bright Baubles (6532); fish Dustwallow Marsh
  (the Artisan quest starts there) or Alterac Mountains, Arathi Highlands, Desolace,
  Stranglethorn Vale, Swamp of Sorrows, Thousand Needles.
- **Artisan (225–300):** quest 6607 *Nat Pagle, Angler Extreme* from Nat Pagle in Dustwallow
  Marsh — level 35 + Fishing 225; catch four quest fish in four zones: **Feralas Ahi**
  (Feralas), **Misty Reed Mahi Mahi** (Swamp of Sorrows), **Sar'theris Striker** (Desolace),
  **Savage Coast Blue Sailfin** (Stranglethorn Vale — item 16969, pulled from the page tail).
- **225–300:** Felwood, Feralas, The Hinterlands, Tanaris, Un'Goro Crater, Western Plaguelands
  — Bright Baubles required (fish escape otherwise).
